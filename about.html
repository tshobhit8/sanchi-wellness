About Sanchi Nutrition & Wellness

Sanchi Nutrition & Wellness was founded on a simple belief: your diet plan should fit your life, not the other way around. As a certified dietitian with over 10 years of experience, I've worked with thousands of clients across weight management, PCOD/PCOS, diabetes, thyroid, and family nutrition — always building plans around real, home-cooked Indian food.

Too many diets fail because they're too restrictive, too generic, or too hard to sustain. My approach is different: personalized nutrition plans backed by clinical expertise, delivered with ongoing support and genuine care, so you can build habits that actually last.

Whether you're looking to lose weight, manage a health condition, or simply eat better, my goal is to make that journey practical, sustainable, and rooted in the foods and culture you already love.
