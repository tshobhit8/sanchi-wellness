The Sanchi Approach to Diabetes Care

Low-GI Indian Diet Plans

Not all carbohydrates affect blood sugar the same way. We build meal plans around low glycemic index staples, smarter grain choices, and portion strategies that keep post-meal sugar spikes in check, without asking you to give up dal-chawal or roti-sabzi.

Personalized to Your Medication and Routine

Whether you're managing Type 2 diabetes, pre-diabetes, or gestational diabetes, your plan accounts for your medication schedule, activity levels, and daily routine, so nutrition works with your treatment, not against it.

Tracking HbA1c and Long-Term Control

We focus on sustainable, long-term blood sugar control rather than short-term fixes. Regular check-ins help track your HbA1c trends and adjust your plan as your body responds.
